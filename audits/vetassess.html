What influences AI search visibility

AI engines don’t rank pages. They build an answer from what they can find and trust about you. Six things decide whether you’re in it.

1Answer pages

Does your site answer the questions customers ask, in plain words an AI can quote?

2Site crawl

Can the AI engines read your site at all? Speed, structure, blocked pages and broken links.

3Citations

Who else mentions you? Directories, industry sites, local media and partners the engines trust.

4Consistency

Is your name, address, services and description identical across your site, Google listing and socials?

5Reviews

How many, how recent, and what they say. Engines read reviews as evidence.

6Structured data

The code behind the page that tells an engine what kind of business you are and what you do.

AI search, explained

What is AI search?

Instead of typing keywords into Google and clicking through ten blue links, people now ask a question in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Perplexity and get one answer. Google itself now puts an AI answer above the results. If that answer names a business, that business gets the call. If it doesn’t name you, you’re invisible to that customer.

How does it work?

AI engines read the web, your Google Business listing, review sites and directories, then write an answer from what they find. They favour businesses that are described clearly and consistently everywhere, have pages that answer the actual question, and get mentioned by other sites. They don’t favour the biggest ad budget.

Isn’t this just SEO?

It overlaps, but it’s not the same. SEO gets you a position on a results page. AI search gives one answer, and either you’re in it or you’re not. A lot of businesses that rank fine on Google are nowhere in AI answers, because the content that wins a ranking isn’t the content an AI can quote. Already have an SEO agency? Keep them. We handle the AI search side and work alongside them. No agency? We do both, Google and AI, in one plan.

Why does the answer change each time?

AI answers aren’t fixed like a ranking. Ask twice and the names shift. That’s why this page is a snapshot and a full audit measures how often you’re mentioned across many runs and many questions, not just one.

How do you measure success?

At kick-off we agree the ten questions your customers actually ask. Then we track how often each AI engine names you on those questions, run many times over, because answers shift. That share of mentions is the score. We report it monthly against the starting point, alongside your Google rankings, so you can see movement engine by engine rather than taking our word for it.

What can I do to fix this?

Most of it is practical work on your own website and listings: a clear description of what you do and where, pages that answer the questions your customers actually ask, consistent details everywhere you appear, reviews, and the behind-the-scenes setup that lets AI engines read your site properly. Some of it you can do yourself. If you would rather it was done for you, that is what we do: we fix it, then re-run the same questions so you can see the change.
